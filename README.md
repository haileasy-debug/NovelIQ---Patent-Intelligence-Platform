NoveliQ — Patent Intelligence Platform
An AI-powered patent intelligence platform that helps inventors and product teams understand whether their invention idea is truly new and how to improve it so it can be patented.
Instead of showing a static list of existing patents, NoveliQ takes your idea as input, compares it against both approved and pending patents, shows where your idea overlaps or differs, and recommends how to adjust your idea to make it more unique and defensible.
Think of it as an AI guide that helps inventors and product teams turn ideas into stronger, patent-ready innovations.

Key capabilities

Takes invention ideas as natural language input

Identifies specific overlaps and points of differentiation

Generates actionable recommendations to strengthen defensibility
